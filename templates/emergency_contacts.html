Emergency Dispatch Protocol
Automated response workflow executed upon critical drowsiness detection

When driver eyes remain closed for 24 cumulative seconds (Warning Level 4), the SafeDrive safety system activates the high-priority emergency protocol:

  • A continuous high-intensity emergency audio alarm sounds in the vehicle cabin.
  • An automated emergency dispatch email is transmitted directly to the configured trusted contact.
  • The alert includes the latest available GPS coordinates and Google Maps routing link for rapid response.
