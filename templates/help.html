About SafeDrive
AI-powered driver drowsiness detection and emergency alert system
v1.0.0
SafeDrive is an intelligent in-cabin driver safety system engineered to prevent fatigue-related motor vehicle accidents. Using edge computer vision and real-time facial landmark detection, SafeDrive continuously evaluates driver alertness, warns the driver before dangerous micro-sleep episodes occur, and coordinates automated emergency dispatch if driver unresponsiveness persists.
How SafeDrive Works
Continuous computer vision pipeline: Camera → Face Mesh → EAR → Escalation
1. Video Acquisition
Live video is acquired through OpenCV webcam feed at 30 frames per second without latency.
2. Face Mesh Landmarking
MediaPipe Face Mesh isolates 468 three-dimensional landmarks, extracting precise eyelid contours for both eyes.
3. EAR Calculation
The Eye Aspect Ratio (EAR) computes the Euclidean distance ratio between vertical and horizontal eye coordinates.
4. Multi-Tier Escalation
When EAR drops below 0.25, timestamps track closure duration, triggering stepped audio warnings and emergency dispatch.
Permissions Help & Browser Site Settings
How to configure Camera and Geolocation access in Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, and other browsers
Camera Access

Camera access is required for real-time computer vision analysis. If the prompt does not appear or was denied:

  1. Click the Lock / Site Settings icon in your browser address bar (beside 127.0.0.1:8000).
  2. Locate Camera permissions.
  3. Toggle from Block to Allow.
  4. Return to SafeDrive and click [Check Camera Permission] in Settings.
Location Access

Geolocation access attaches real-time vehicle GPS coordinates to emergency emails. If permission was denied:

  1. Click the Lock / Site Settings icon in your browser address bar.
  2. Locate Location permissions.
  3. Toggle to Allow.
  4. Return to SafeDrive and click [Check Location Permission] in Settings.
Note: Browser native permission prompts and controls may differ slightly between Chrome, Edge, and operating system permission managers.

Emergency Alerts Protocol
Automated response executed when driver eye closure exceeds 24 seconds

When unresponsiveness persists through 24 seconds of continuous eye closure, SafeDrive immediately initiates emergency protocols:

  • Acoustic Cabin Alarm: A high-frequency alarm (3500 Hz for 5000 ms) plays at maximum volume to awaken the driver.
  • Automated Emergency Email: An urgent notification is dispatched in the background to the configured emergency contact email (or primary safety recipient).
  • GPS Coordinates: The notification includes real-time vehicle GPS coordinates and direct Google Maps / navigation links to enable immediate roadside assistance.
  • Safety Event Log: A full incident record is preserved in the database for post-trip driver safety review.
